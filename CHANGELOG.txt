Prayer Times Desktop v2.5.3
Built on v2.4.0. English and Bangla throughout.

- Astrolabe: the 24-hour local solar scale sits in its own band between the degree limb and the stereographic tympan. The plate is clipped so the horizon and coordinate grid no longer cross the hour numerals.
- During sunrise, Zawal, or the interval before sunset, the clock page’s main countdown counts the prohibited time, not the prayer that happens to still be open.
- Qur’an 23:1 (Al-Mu’minun) still marks completing all five Fard. It now appears in its own gold-and-silver card, then leaves. Settings has a separate “All five Fard verse” timer (default 12 seconds), apart from Reward seconds.
- Home cards for the five prayers show the Fard circle in performance order with Sunnah and Nafl, the same way the completion record does. Mark Complete stays. Cards size to their content and wrap into leftover space.
- Voluntary home cards keep their own rak‘ah circles (that is how a count is recorded). They do not gain a Fard circle. Sunnah Rawatib still has no separate start time.
- Reminder boxes have a small inset so the label is not flush with the border.
- A prayer title opens a page of the Qur’an and hadith already stored for that prayer. Nothing new is invented; the page stops when the stored passages do.
- Preferred-time countdown and its note are larger on the clock page and on the event countdown.
- “Remind me before a prayer begins” now includes voluntary prayers that have their own start: Ishraq/Duha, Awwabin, Qiyam, Tahajjud, Witr, and, on the days they occur, Tarawih and Eid. Rawatib is not listed.
- The daily record opens with a short gold-and-silver card of the rak‘ahs recorded that day.
- Version 2.5.3.

Prayer Times Desktop v2.4.0
Built on v2.3.0. English and Bangla throughout.

- Tray hover: no title line. Available events show time left, then the next event and its countdown (hours and minutes, or minutes only in the last hour), in 127 characters or fewer, with Bangla digits in Bangla mode.
- Rotate and Flip use the same smooth brushed-silver ring as the settings crown.
- Voluntary ticks fill in order; later circles are locked and dimmed, and only the last ticked circle can be unticked. Saved gaps are packed from the left, keeping the total rak‘ahs.
- Witr shows odd totals 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11. A “More than 11?” box accepts 13, 15, 17 and up. Old 2+1 records become 3.
- Other voluntary “More than 12?” boxes accept 14, 16, 18 and up. Invalid input gets a bilingual hint naming the nearest valid numbers. Bangla digits are accepted.
- The old milestone reward engine and the per-prayer verse or hadith on voluntary Mark Complete are replaced by one phrase per tick, for the highest-ranked kind recorded (Fard, then Sunnah, then Nafl, then Voluntary): La ilaha illallah, Allahu Akbar, Alhamdulillah or SubhanAllah, each with Arabic, pronunciation and meaning. One “Reward seconds” setting (default 8) and a “Show reward text” switch.
- Once all five Fard are done, the progress disc shows Qur’an 23:1 (قَدْ أَفْلَحَ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ) in Ruqaa, even if nothing else is recorded. Pronunciation, meaning and reference sit in the caption.
- The progress circle is centred in the left third of Today’s progress; the text and legend stay in the other two-thirds.
- The progress line now reacts to voluntary prayers (for example “Qiyam done, 6 rak‘ahs.”), the summary shows a Voluntary rak‘ah count instead of “out of 5”, and a few awkward templates were reworded.
- Fonts and dead-code cleanup are deferred to v2.5.0.
- Version 2.4.0.

Prayer Times Desktop v2.1.1
- Forbidden times (sunrise, Zawal, before sunset) are now a calm "pause" card instead of a violet alert: the same dark steel finish as the other cards with a thin muted amber edge and no icon. A small label ("Pause · Zawal"), the headline "Prayer resumes at 11:48", the time range, an inner countdown card styled like Next Event, a thin progress bar that fills as the window passes, and a "Next: Dhuhr · 11:48" line. English and Bangla; text wraps at every size.
- Astrolabe: a plain click (no drag) opens a large astrolabe page with a smooth move, like the clock; dragging still rotates and never opens it (a double-click still resets and waits 0.26 s so it is not mistaken for a click). The live clock moves into the top bar. Everything the small instrument used to print is shown once in cards: sun height and direction, moon position, phase and illumination, the three pause windows, current and next prayer, coordinates (with local sidereal time) and the instrument controls. Back returns home.
- Enlarged astrolabe detail (only when the instrument is at least about 330 px wide): 1-degree limb scale with degree numerals, five-minute hour marks, finer altitude and azimuth divisions with numerals and cardinal points, engine-turned plate hairlines, 1-degree ecliptic divisions, engraved rete and named star pointers that stay upright. English and Bangla text only.
- Coin flip: a machined flip button on the astrolabe (home and expanded page) turns it over in a 3D coin-toss animation to a mirrored view (printed words stay readable, dragging follows the hand). Flip and rotation are independent: Home / double-click resets rotation only. Both are saved and restored after a restart.
- Rewards: the phrase and the voluntary reward text were drawn inside the small progress disc and were squeezed until they overlapped and were clipped; Fard phrases also never cleared when their time ran out. They now appear in a readable reward panel beside the disc (a voluntary prayer shows its own reward text), and every reward repaints itself away exactly when its recognition time ends. The disc keeps showing the count and the Alhamdulillah mark.
- Version 2.1.1.

Prayer Times Desktop v2.0.1
- Bangla: every string now follows the language switch. Fixed leftovers in the keyboard-shortcut dialog, the Mark Complete button, toasts, reminder selects and labels, the countdown line, the time-zone line (now "বাংলাদেশ সময়" style) and the astrolabe star tooltips. Bengali digits everywhere, and no letter-spacing on Bangla text (it broke conjuncts such as "আসট্রোলেব").
- Cards: Qur’an first, hadith only when no verse exists, then other notes. Added verified verses for Duha, Tarawih, Eid, Witr, Rawatib, Tahiyyat al-Masjid, Istikhara, eclipse, rain and voluntary nafl. The five Daily prayers notes are Qur’anic for every school.
- Astrolabe: redrawn as a planispheric astrolabe in traditional orientation: mater with throne and shackle, 360 degree limb, 24-hour solar scale, plate with tropics, equator, almucantars, azimuths, horizon and twilight lines, and a rotating rete with ecliptic band, twelve signs, star pointers and an alidade pointing at the sun. The rete turns with real sidereal time; the sun and moon sit at their true declination and hour angle and are drawn at their true apparent-size ratio.
- Panel: astrolabe 60 % | clock 40 % in one row. Both cards have the same height, which follows the window (viewport-relative), and the clock face is never larger than the astrolabe. Labels sit only above and below the instrument.
- Main clock: modern analog face with brass bezel, numerals, a second hand that glides every frame, the local time and the full Gregorian and Hijri date inside the same card.
- Top bar: one row. Icon and app name at the far left; the location card at the far right reads exactly "coordinates: (lat, lon)" (Bangla: "স্থানাঙ্ক: (...)"). New astrolabe + mihrab icon for the header, favicon, tray and Windows icons.
- Mark Complete: a square, smoothly rounded button directly under the main tick of every available event. The white tick only records. Mark Complete records the main tick, leaves untouched sub-ticks as they are, closes the card and the next card slides into its place.
- Undo prayer: removed from every homepage card (including the voluntary card). Clicking a day in Weekly Prayer Progress opens that day; every recorded card there has Undo prayer at the bottom right, same shape in the opposite colours.
- Voluntary: the undo-rak‘ah / 0-nafl strip is gone; the sixth card (Other voluntary, +2 rak‘ahs) completes the grid and only adds rak‘ahs. Sets show in the running event card and in the Undo prayer edit, not in Additional worship. Legend says "Voluntary".
- Reward: the recognition phrases (Shukran Lillah ... Allahu Akbar) and Alhamdulillah were squeezed to unreadable sizes by the compact layout (disc 3.4 rem, 0.4 rem text). The disc is back to its original 8.25 rem maximum and its text scales with the disc. Phrases, thresholds and Good Job / Great Job logic are unchanged and Bangla works.
- No-scroll opening view: title, location, astrolabe + clock, available events, next event and today’s progress fit the window height. Sizes follow the viewport; if many events are open, the page scales itself down as a last resort (checked at 1920x1080 to 420x700).
